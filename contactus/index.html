Common questions

A tag won't scan

Hold the top of your iPhone flat against the tag for a second or two. Tags can't be read through metal, so take the bag out of a metal bin first. If a tag is damaged, you can still find and manage that bag by hand in the app.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings and choose Delete Account. Your account and milk storage data are removed from our systems within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.
