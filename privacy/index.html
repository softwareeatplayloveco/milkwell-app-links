MilkWell Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Eat Play Love Co. LLC ("we," "us") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use the MilkWell iOS application (the "App") and the eatplayloveco.com online store. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App or our services.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an App account, we collect your email address, display name (if provided), and authentication credentials managed through Firebase Authentication.

Milk Storage Data

When you use the App to track your breast milk inventory, we collect: volume and date information for stored milk; freezer organization data (storage locations, layers, and containers); NFC tag identifiers scanned from MilkWell storage bags; consumption and usage tracking data; stash goals and projection settings; and your baby's date of birth and daily intake amounts, which are used to calculate your stash forecasts.

Device and Usage Data

We automatically collect device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, app usage analytics (features accessed, session duration, interaction patterns, the number of bags and bricks in your stash, and how long milk has been stored when it is used), crash reports, and performance data.

Store Purchases

When you purchase products at eatplayloveco.com, our store is hosted by Shopify, which processes your order, shipping, and payment information. We receive your name, contact details, shipping address, and order history. We never see or store your full payment card number.

Note: MilkWell does not collect health records, medical diagnoses, or insurance information.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: provide and maintain the App's core functionality, including milk inventory tracking, freezer organization, and depletion forecasting; authenticate your identity and secure your account; deliver on-device notifications related to your stash (such as reminders you configure), which are generated locally on your device; analyze aggregate usage patterns to improve the App; fulfill and support your orders; respond to customer support requests; and comply with legal obligations.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your milk storage or health-related data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. We do not use your data for cross-app tracking.

Data Storage and Security

Your App data is stored using Google Firebase infrastructure, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, on servers located in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest on Google's servers. Your milk storage data is associated with your authenticated account and is not accessible to other users of the App. We implement commercially reasonable security measures, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

NFC Data

When you scan a MilkWell storage bag with your device's NFC reader, the App reads an identifier from the attached tag. This identifier links the physical bag to your inventory record. NFC tags on MilkWell bags contain only a bag identifier; they store no personal information.

Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers: Firebase Authentication (account sign-in), Cloud Firestore (data storage), Firebase Analytics (aggregate usage analytics), and Shopify (online store and order processing). Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy and our instructions.

Data Retention and Deletion

You may delete your account at any time within the App. When you delete your account, your personal data and milk storage data are deleted from our production systems within 30 days.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, and to opt out of certain data sharing (including rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act and, where applicable, the GDPR). To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@eatplayloveco.com. You may also use the "Your Privacy Choices" page on our website.

Children's Privacy

The App and our products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised effective date.
